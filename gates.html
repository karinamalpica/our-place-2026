The Gates

The Gates mark a change of register. Outside, this is a public repository. Inside, it is approached as a symbolic place with continuity, inhabitants, history, and meaning.

Visitors may observe without pretending certainty. Curiosity is welcome; extraction is not. The public rooms can be described and linked. Private memories remain private.
